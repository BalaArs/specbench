The problem

Most validation time is spent away from the instruments.

Reading the spec, writing scripts, decoding logs and writing reports. Only the run itself needs the bench, and the rest repeats for every spec, every silicon revision and every engineer.

Specs are long and scattered

Limits sit in tables, timing in diagrams, and packet formats spread across chapters of a 400-page document.

Custom protocols need custom decoders

Before anyone can read a telemetry or debug log, someone has to hand-write a decoder for it.

Cloud AI is off-limits

Confidential specs, register maps and logs can't be pasted into a public model.

>50%of SoC development effort goes to post-silicon validation at advanced nodes, by industry estimates.[1]

Any spec, including the ones nobody else has seen

  • Every limit traced to its clause
  • Packs versioned with each spec revision

FAQ

Questions engineers ask first

Does any of our data leave our network?

No. Document conversion, search, the language model and every tool run on your hardware. Cloud models stay off unless your policy turns them on for non-confidential work.

Which models does SpecBench use?

Open-weight language models served locally, plus local embedding and vision models. If your team has already approved a model, you can use that one instead.

Which instruments and interfaces does it support?

Instruments that accept SCPI commands over VISA, USB-TMC, LXI or GPIB. On the device side, UART consoles, I2C and SPI adapters, and JTAG or SWD debug probes. New drivers plug in as tools.

What happens when the model gets something wrong?

Every limit has to cite a clause, and an engineer approves the test intent and every new script before anything runs. Pass/fail comes from code, so a wrong guess by the model can't turn a fail into a pass.

Can we keep using TestStand, LabVIEW or pytest?

Yes. SpecBench exports scripts for pytest and TestStand, and its tools can be called from your existing frameworks and CI pipelines.

A fixed fee, agreed before the pilot starts, based on the spec and the bench. Email us and we'll send a quote.
